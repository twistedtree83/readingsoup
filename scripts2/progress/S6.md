# S6 — Condition: Mudsound and the grapheme engine

Issue: #29 · Branch: `sprint/soup-mvp` · Status: **shipped**

## What shipped

`src/core/graphemes.js` — a 14-rule substitution table swapping common graphemes
for rarer but entirely legitimate English spellings of the same sound. A subset
is chosen per passage from the seed and applied consistently throughout it.

Real output at seed 3:

> Any resident who observes a wombat crossing a public **foutpath** between nine
> and five must log the **enkountur** with the Parks Office within two working
> days, and must not, **undur** any **cirkumstances**, wave.

## Decisions taken

- **Legitimate orthography, never symbols.** `þ`/`ʃ` would make it a cipher —
  undecodable, and it would teach that reading difficulty is a code. The output
  must stay sound-out-able. A test applies every rule to fourteen probe words and
  asserts letters-only output.
- **Rules are context-safe.** Only swaps that preserve the sound: `qu→kw`,
  `ck→k`, `x→ks`, `tion→shun`, `ee→ea`, `ai→ay`, `oo→ou`, `f↔ph`, final `y→ie`,
  final `er→ur`, hard `c→k`, `wh→w`, voiced final `s→z`. Rejected `sh→ci` and
  `k→ch`: both are legal English but a reader defaults to the wrong phoneme
  word-initially, which tips the round from hard into unreadable.
- **`f` and `ph` are declared conflicting** and never both chosen — they invert
  each other and would round-trip to the original.
- **Consistent within a passage.** The reader learns the mapping mid-read and
  accelerates toward the end. That acceleration is what makes the round
  finishable rather than cruel, and it gives the round a satisfying shape.
- **Proper nouns and the closing clause are excluded.** Sentence-opening
  capitals are correctly not treated as proper nouns. The closing clause is
  everything after the final comma if it falls within the last 8 tokens,
  otherwise the last 4 — so the comic payoff always survives.
- **In solo, `Read it to them` reveals the clean passage.** The true handover to
  another participant is S18.

## Test correction

The first draft asserted on each rule's replacement template and failed, because
the voiced-`s` rule legitimately carries a `$1` backreference. Rewritten to
assert on **output** across probe words — behaviour, not implementation.
