# S17 — Tag in

Issue: #40 · Branch: `main` · Status: **shipped**

## What shipped

The reader can hand their passage to a colleague at any point in the round. The
tagged phone becomes the reader immediately — same barrier, same passage, from
the start. The clock does not restart, the room does not get its plays back, and
it chains as many times as the room needs.

## The wording is half the feature

This is one of the PRD's four hard wellbeing requirements, and the requirement
is not "a handover exists" — it is that handing over reads as **a move in the
game, never as giving up**.

- The control says **"Tag someone in"**, and it is folded away behind a summary
  so it is never the loudest thing on a struggling reader's screen. *"I've
  finished reading"* stays the primary button.
- Opening it says *"They pick it up from the start, same passage. The cards stay
  live."* — what happens next, not what just failed.
- The projector reads **"Tagged in / Bea / Same passage, same barrier, from the
  top."** The person who handed it over **is not named on the slide at all**.
  Asserted: their name appears nowhere in the host view.

## Decisions taken

- **No accept step.** The reader taps a name and that phone is the reader. An
  accept prompt is slow, and a decline would be a public refusal to help a
  struggling colleague — the exact opposite of the framing this needs. There is
  no pending state anywhere for one to hang off.
- **The seats swap, and so do the hands.** This is not tidiness. The tagged
  colleague might be the only person holding the card that lifts this barrier;
  if they took their hand into the reader's seat, the room would be left with a
  barrier it could not remove. Passing the hand back the other way keeps exactly
  the same cards on the table, so [[S15]]'s guarantee survives a handover.
  Asserted directly: tag in the holder of the fix, across five seeds, and the
  fix is still on the table.
- **Spent cards travel with the hand.** A card is spent for the *round*, so
  moving hands must not make a spent one playable again.
- **From the start, not mid-passage.** A partial handoff is fiddly to reason
  about and unreadable on a projector. The passage re-renders whole, including
  restarting the Vanishing's expiry timers, and a typed round hands over an
  empty keyboard rather than half a sentence.
- **`startedAt`, `locked`, `plays` and `helped` are untouched.** The watch window
  belongs to the round, not the reader: cards stay live across the handover.
- **Nobody meets the same barrier twice**, so anyone who has already had this
  condition is off the list — the room would be watching a practised
  performance, and the tagged colleague would learn nothing. Same rule
  [[S14]] applies to spotlight selection, applied at the other door into the
  reader's seat.
- **Observers are never on the list.** They are never called on.
- **Names travel, tokens do not.** The reader's phone gets a list of names and
  sends back a position, which the shell resolves — the same trick the host
  roster uses, for the same reason: a token on somebody else's phone would let
  them act as that person. Asserted that no token appears in the reader's view.
- **An already-lifted barrier travels with the passage.** If a card landed
  before the tag, the new reader inherits the accommodated render rather than
  being handed the barrier back.

## Verified

220 unit tests: condition and passage transfer intact, the window and play count
unaffected, chaining five deep, hands and spent cards swapping with the seats,
the fix still on the table after tagging in its holder across five seeds, and
observers plus anyone who has had this barrier absent from the list. Solo has no
tag control at all.

Harness green at 1, 2, 5, 8 and 20, tagging on the wire after the window opens:
the seat moves, the plays left are unchanged, the cards stay unlocked, the old
reader is moved to watching with a hand and the new one holds none, and whoever
is holding it can finish. In the browser: *"Tag someone in →"* sitting under the
finish button on a Soup passage, a list of four eligible colleagues, and the
projector announcing Bea without naming Priya.
