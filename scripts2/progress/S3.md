# S3 — Condition: Fog

Issue: #26 · Branch: `sprint/soup-mvp` · Status: **shipped**

## What shipped

The passage renders at very low contrast against the paper ground. Words are
untouched. `Change the colours` snaps to full contrast in one action.

## Decision taken: the content/presentation split

Fog broke the blanket invariant S1 wrote — *mangled output never reconstructs the
source* — because Fog does not alter the text at all. Rather than weaken the
assertion, it was split in two, which is more honest and matches the Q1 decision:

- `CONTENT_TRANSFORMING` (Soup, Mudsound) — rendered text must never reconstruct
  the source.
- Presentational (Fog, Slippery Floor, The Vanishing) — the words genuinely are
  there; the barrier is *how they are shown*.
- Both — every condition's render mode must differ from its accommodated render.

The privacy boundary sits at the non-reader line, not between a reader and the
passage they are meant to be reading. A presentational barrier that hid the text
from its own reader's DOM would be defending against the wrong threat.

## Notes

- `render.contrast` is `1` for clean, `config.fog.contrast` (0.09) for fogged.
  The client maps it through `color-mix` against the paper ground.
- Chrome stays at full contrast while the passage is fogged — verified in-browser.
  This is what makes the barrier read as deliberate rather than as a broken app,
  and it is why the design system's high-contrast baseline is load-bearing.
