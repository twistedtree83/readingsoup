# S5 — Condition: The Vanishing

Issue: #28 · Branch: `sprint/soup-mvp` · Status: **shipped**

## What shipped

Each word carries an expiry. The client **removes the node** when it fires.
`Chunk it` strips every expiry and delivers the passage in stable blocks.

Verified in-browser: 32 tokens → 29 → 19 as expiries fired, then back to 32 in
6 chunks after the accommodation, with no further removal.

## Decisions taken

- **Removal, never opacity.** A test asserts the emitted instruction contains
  neither `opacity` nor `visibility`. This is the one condition where a
  struggling reader has real motive to inspect the page, and `.remove()` costs
  the same as a fade.
- **Expiry staggers forward** — token *i* expires at `fadeAfterMs + i * staggerMs`.
  The core cannot know where the reader's eye is, so the stagger approximates a
  reading rate: it trails just behind someone moving at the target pace and
  overtakes someone slower. Being overtaken is the experience worth having.
- **Time enters the client, not the core.** `mangle` emits a duration; the shell
  owns `setTimeout`. The purity gate would have rejected any other arrangement.
- Timers are cleared on every re-render, so accommodating mid-round genuinely
  stops the vanishing rather than leaving orphaned timers to eat the fixed text.

## Notes

`render.chunkSize` is 0 for every other condition, so the chunk path is inert
elsewhere. Tokens now carry `data-id` in the DOM so the client can find and
remove them individually.
