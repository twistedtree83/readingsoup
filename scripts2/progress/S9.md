# S9 — Solo tour complete: six conditions and the personal catalogue

Issue: #32 · Branch: `sprint/soup-mvp` · Status: **shipped**

## What shipped

The complete solo experience. All six conditions in sequence, full deck each
round, unlimited attempts, no timing, no score — ending on the catalogue.

Verified in-browser, all six rounds in one run:

| # | Condition | Observed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Soup | `gaps=false` |
| 2 | Fog | text colour `srgb(0.89 0.88 0.84)` on paper |
| 3 | Slippery Floor | tokens carrying `translateY` |
| 4 | The Vanishing | tokens expiring |
| 5 | Mudsound | substituted graphemes |
| 6 | Butterfingers | typing input |

Catalogue: six items, six marked, correct names and cards.

## Decisions taken

- **Order is a ramp, not a shuffle.** Soup first (the simplest to grasp), the
  visual conditions through the middle, Butterfingers last because it is
  different in kind and lands hardest as a contrast to five reading barriers.
  Randomising would sometimes open on the typing task, which reads as a
  different app rather than as a twist.
- **A quiet "3 of 6" position indicator, no clock.** It answers "how much is
  left" without measuring the participant. Position is not duration.
- **The catalogue is per-device and per-participant.** `viewFor` marks only the
  requesting participant's own conditions, and a test asserts another
  participant's view carries no `had: true` at all. This is the projection the
  multiplayer reveal (S20) will reuse unchanged — the host screen gets the same
  catalogue with nothing marked, and attribution happens by hands up in the room.
- **Descriptions are written from the inside** — what the barrier feels like,
  not what it is called clinically. The heading is "what you can do on Monday",
  so the whole screen reads as action rather than diagnosis.
- **"Start again" reseeds**, so a second run draws different passages.
