# S13 — The silent round

Issue: #36 · Branch: `main` · Status: **shipped**

## What shipped

The facilitator taps *Start — everyone at once*. Every active participant
receives a condition at the same moment, privately, on their own phone. The
projector shows a countdown and nothing else. No turn announcements, no
audience, nobody reading aloud.

**The whole room gets the same passage.** Identical forty words, five different
reasons they are hard. That is what isolates the variable, and it is the
strongest line the facilitator gets: *we all read the same words and I could
barely start while you finished fine.*

## Why this exists

One-round-per-person does not scale — twenty rounds at two minutes each is forty
minutes of the same game, and cutting it short would leave people visibly blank
on the reveal. Separating *feeling a barrier* from *diagnosing one* lets
everybody get the first in ninety seconds. It also means a participant's first
encounter with their barrier is private rather than in front of nineteen
colleagues, which is a completely different emotional experience.

## Decisions taken

- **Observers get the same passage rendered clean**, flagged `observerCover`,
  with *"read this, then look up and watch the room."* Head down over a phone,
  physically indistinguishable from everyone else. An observer sitting with
  nothing to do discloses through body language what the app carefully keeps
  private — and it is genuinely useful, since they get the baseline at normal
  difficulty to compare against.
- **Butterfingers is excluded** via `SILENT_POOL`. It is a typed task whose
  contrast lands solely when the room watches it; done silently it is a person
  typing badly at nobody.
- **Even distribution by construction**, not by luck: round-robin over a
  seeded shuffle of the five reading conditions, so max and min counts differ by
  at most one. Tested at 20 participants.
- **Time is an input.** The shell ticks once a second and the core only ever
  subtracts, so the countdown is deterministic in tests and the purity gate
  stays green. The ticker runs only while a silent round is live.
- **The host view carries a countdown and nothing else** — no roster, no names,
  no per-person progress. Asserted.

## What the silent round must still guarantee

The spotlight rule — *no non-reader receives passage content* — does not apply
here, because everybody legitimately holds their own copy and there is no reader
to be a non-reader of. The harness now exempts silent-phase views from that
check and asserts the rule that actually governs this phase instead: **your
phone tells you nothing about anyone else.** No other participant's name, no
assignment map, no condition names.

That distinction is recorded because weakening the leak check without replacing
it would have quietly removed the project's most important assertion.

## Known gap, assigned to S21

A participant who joins *during* a silent round has no assignment, so they fall
through to observer cover: they get the clean passage and never meet a barrier.
Not a privacy problem, but "everyone active meets a barrier" does not hold for
late arrivals. S21 owns the private catch-up round.

## Verified

138 unit tests. Harness green at 1, 2, 5, 8, 20 — one passage across the whole
room, observers covered, no turn announcements, countdown reaching zero, and the
room returning to the roster afterwards. In-browser: projector countdown ticking
on ink, and a participant's phone showing Soup with *"Nobody is watching.
Nothing is being timed."*
