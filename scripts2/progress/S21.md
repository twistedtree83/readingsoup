# S21 — Late joiners and mid-session opt-out

Issue: #44 · Branch: `main` · Status: **shipped**

## What shipped

Two doors, in and out, both of which have to cost nothing in front of the room.

**Late joiners** get the silent round privately, on arrival. Ninety seconds at
their own desk on the **same forty words the room had**, while everyone else is
mid-spotlight. Then they drop into the card game and are eligible for spotlights
like anyone else.

**Mid-session opt-out** is a persistent, quiet control on every phone —
*"Watch from here instead"* — that converts a participant to observer silently
and permanently. They keep their hand and go on playing.

## The two invariants this slice is really about

**Nothing moves when somebody steps out.** Asserted byte-for-byte: the host view
and every other participant's view are identical strings before and after an
opt-out, and the reducer emits no effect at all. The only screen that changes is
the one in that person's own hand.

**A reader opting out is indistinguishable from finishing.** Tapping it mid-turn
sets `finished` exactly as *done* would, and the conversion happens behind it.
Asserted by building the same round twice — once ended with `DONE`, once with
`OPT_OUT` — and comparing the host view and every other phone as strings. A
visible withdrawal mid-round is precisely the public moment the observer role
exists to prevent.

## Decisions taken

- **The control never changes shape.** It is on every phone, in every phase, for
  participants and observers alike, and tapping it as an observer does nothing.
  A control that appears, disappears, or turns into a confirmation is a control
  that tells the next seat along what you just did with it. What changes after
  tapping is the rest of your own screen: *"You're watching from here. Cards
  still work."* in the same place, at the same weight. That line is on your own
  device only, which is the same line the PRD already draws — an observer's own
  phone has always known its own role.
- **Permanent, with no route back.** A toggle would make it a decision to
  revisit rather than a door out, and would put the person back in the pool the
  moment they mis-tapped. `JOIN` now refuses to un-observe someone, so a
  reconnect or a second pass through the join screen cannot undo it either.
- **They keep their hand.** Card play carries no exposure and needs no reading;
  taking it away would turn a quiet exit into a demotion.
- **The session's shape is frozen when the facilitator starts.** This began as a
  correctness tidy-up and turned out to be a real leak: with the round budget
  computed live, a six-person room dropping to five would have flipped the
  projector from a spotlight chooser to *"2 rounds each"* at the exact moment
  somebody opted out. `START_SESSION` now stores mode, budget and rounds-per-
  person, and both the reducer and the projector read that. A late arrival
  cannot reshape the session either.
- **The catch-up is the room's own passage.** *"We all read the same forty
  words"* is the facilitator's strongest line, and it should still be true for
  the person who walked in late.
- **Their barrier is whichever the room has met least**, so a late arrival
  widens coverage instead of piling onto whatever was already common — and it
  counts as one they have had, so a later spotlight will not hand it back.
- **Catch-ups tick independently of the phase.** They run on their own clocks
  alongside whatever the room is doing, so a latecomer reads privately through
  somebody else's spotlight. They are also not dealt into a round that started
  before they arrived.
- **A reconnect is not an arrival.** The catch-up only starts for a token the
  room has never seen; a phone that locks and comes back keeps the one it had.
- **No catch-up where there was no silent round.** A small room never had one,
  so a latecomer there simply starts playing.

## Verified

276 unit tests: the private catch-up on the room's own passage with a real
barrier recorded as seen; the countdown running down and handing them back;
eligibility for spotlights afterwards, minus the barrier they just met; a
catch-up running through somebody else's spotlight without either noticing; a
reconnect not restarting one; no catch-up before the session starts or in a
small room. For the opt-out: the control present on every phone before and
after, permanence across a rejoin, the projector and every other phone
unchanged as strings, no effect emitted, the hand kept and still playable, never
being called on again, the catalogue still marking what they met, mid-silent
conversion handing them the clean cover, and reader-conversion proved identical
to finishing.

Harness green at 1, 2, 5, 8 and 20: a latecomer joining a live room gets a
catch-up while the room's phase does not move and no other phone hears about it;
opting out leaves the projector and every other phone byte-identical and emits
nothing. In the browser: *"Watch from here instead"* sitting quietly at the foot
of the phone, and the same slot afterwards reading *"You're watching from here.
Cards still work."*
