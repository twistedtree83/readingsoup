# S8 — Passage library

Issue: #31 · Branch: `sprint/soup-mvp` · Status: **shipped**

## What shipped

20 reading passages and 10 dictation prompts. Municipal wombat regulations, a
haunted kettle, a bench with terms and conditions printed on its underside, a
museum exhibit on loan to itself, a bus that does not run on any day following a
day on which it did not run.

## Constraints held, and tested

- **Everyday vocabulary throughout** — asserted as no word over 13 letters. If
  the words themselves are hard, the activity simulates a vocabulary gap, which
  is a different barrier and not one of the six.
- **The payoff sits in the final clause.** Not machine-testable, but written to
  it throughout — the absurdity has to arrive last or there is no reason to
  finish, and finishing is the point.
- **Length banded by condition.** Short band (28–38 words) for Soup and
  Mudsound, where a wall of unspaced or respelt text punishes at length; full
  band (38–52) for the visual conditions. Ten in each band, so neither runs out.
- **Nothing about children, schools, disability or assessment** — asserted by
  regex over both sets. Bureaucratic and domestic absurdity only.
- Every item ends on terminal punctuation, so the payoff has actually landed.

## Fixes made during the slice

Six passages missed their band on first draft — `shelf` and `recipe` ran over
the short band, `wombat`, `kettle`, `roundabout` and `hotel` fell under the full
band. The passages were edited rather than the bands widened: a "short" passage
longer than a "full" one defeats the point of banding.

## Test correction

The privacy suite was matching tells like `answer` as **substrings** of the
serialised view, and the word `answering.` in the vending-machine passage
tripped it. Rewritten to walk the object and check **keys** — which is what the
rule actually is. A passage containing the word "answering" is not a leak.

## Notes

Selection is now seeded, so sessions vary while still never repeating within
one. `byId` resolves both passages and dictation prompts, since a typing round
stores its prompt id in the same field.
